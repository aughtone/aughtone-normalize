# Research

Investigations: the question that came up, the trail of options weighed and dead ends walked far enough to rule out, what was found, and what to do about it. A design being worked out by discussion lives here too, for as long as it is still moving.

Keep enough of the trail that a future reader trusts the conclusion — including the options that lost, since looking up an approach the project did *not* take is the most common reason anyone opens one of these.

**Not here:** the settled rules that came out of an investigation, once they have hardened — those become a [Specification](../specifications/README.md), a [Decision](../decisions/README.md), or both, and this record links to them.
