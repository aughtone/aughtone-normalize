# Architecture Decision Records

One hard-to-reverse choice per record: the forces that applied, the option taken, and what was rejected and why. Written when the decision is made rather than afterwards, and **append-only** — a record that turns out wrong is superseded by a new one that says so, never edited into agreement with the present.

Anything that changes a published canonical form belongs here, because that class of change cannot be undone: consumers have already derived and stored tokens from the old bytes.

**Not here:** how something currently works (that is [Specifications](Specifications)), or an investigation that has not settled on a choice yet (that is [Research](Research)). A recommendation only becomes a decision when someone commits to it.
