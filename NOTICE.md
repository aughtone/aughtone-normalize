The frozen tables under `unicode/` and the conformance corpus used to test them are generated from the Unicode Character Database, the pinned copy of which is checked in under `ucd/`. That data is:

It is distributed under the Unicode Terms of Use, available at https://www.unicode.org/terms_of_use.html, and the data files carry the Unicode License v3. Redistribution of the data, and of tables generated from it, carries that notice with it.

The data is reproduced here, unmodified and checksummed, so that a build is reproducible without reaching the network. Generated tables are derived from it mechanically by `tools/ucd-generator`.
